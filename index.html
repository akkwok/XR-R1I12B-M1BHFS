A 3U Compute Tray Built for Flexibility and Serviceability

Each 3U compute tray pairs 8 NVIDIA Rubin GPUs with dual Intel® Xeon® 6776P processors in a hybrid-cooled design — direct liquid cooling for the GPU sled and air cooling for the CPU sled — delivering a more easy-maintained thermal solution than full liquid cooling. This fully cable-less, board-to-board architecture and simplifies assembly, while generous storage space give customers flexible storage options for U.2/E1.S configurations. The fully air-cooled CPU sled also cuts maintenance time by up to 50%, striking the ideal balance of performance and operational simplicity so enterprises can scale AI Factories with fewer components and less downtime.

FAQs

  • Why does XR R1I12B-M1BHFS  use a 3U hybrid-cooling design instead of full liquid cooling like the 2U XR R1I12B-M1BHFS ?
    Why does XR R1I12B-M1BHFS  use a 3U hybrid-cooling design instead of full liquid cooling like the 2U XR R1I12B-M1BHFS ? XA NR1I-E12L is built for data centers that need to run HGX Rubin NVL8 GPU compute but aren't yet ready for full direct liquid cooling (DLC) across every component. It applies direct liquid cooling to the GPU sled only, while the CPU sled uses air cooling (6 x system fans). This hybrid architecture, housed in a 3U (447mm x 800mm x 131mm) chassis with 54V busbar power, lets customers adopt Rubin NVL8 GPU density while reducing the facility-side liquid-cooling infrastructure required at initial deployment.
  • Can XR R1I12B-M1BHFS  be deployed in an existing air-cooled data center?
    Can XR R1I12B-M1BHFS  be deployed in an existing air-cooled data center? Not entirely — the HGX Rubin NVL8 GPUs always require liquid cooling regardless of chassis design. XR R1I12B-M1BHFS 's hybrid-cooling architecture minimizes the liquid-cooling footprint by confining it to the GPU sled, while the CPU sled and other components remain air-cooled. Customers still need CDU and liquid-supply infrastructure for the GPU loop, so facility cooling capacity and readiness should be assessed before deployment. ASUS can help evaluate whether an existing environment can support this hybrid configuration.
  • What storage flexibility does XR R1I12B-M1BHFS offer compared to the E1.S-based XR R1I12B-M1BFS ?
    What storage flexibility does XR R1I12B-M1BHFS offer compared to the E1.S-based XR R1I12B-M1BFS ? XA NR1I-E12L provides 8 x 2.5" hot-swap NVMe drive bays (with E1.S support reserved as a design option), offering more capacity and format flexibility than the LR's fixed 8 x E1.S (4TB) configuration. The larger 2.5" bay footprint gives customers more options when sizing local storage for data-intensive AI workloads.
  • What are the power and cooling requirements per rack for XR R1I12B-M1BHFS ?
    What are the power and cooling requirements per rack for XR R1I12B-M1BHFS ? A full 48RU MGX rack supports 9 XA NR1I-E12L compute trays, totaling 72 NVIDIA Rubin GPUs and 18 Intel Xeon 6776P processors. Each compute tray draws approximately 25.7kW (22.2kW GPU + 3.5kW CPU incl. fan), for a rack-level system power budget of approximately 237.2kW. Power is delivered through 2+2 redundant 110 kW power shelves (415–480VAC) with 8 x 100A IEC60309 power whips, and a 5000A liquid-cooled rack busbar. Cooling is hybrid — liquid for the GPU sled, air for the CPU sled (liquid CPU cooling under evaluation).
  • What deployment and after-sales support does ASUS provide for global rollouts of XR R1I12B-M1BHFS ?

Deployment and after-sales service options may vary by region and project requirements. ASUS teams can work with customers to define the appropriate service scope for each deployment.
